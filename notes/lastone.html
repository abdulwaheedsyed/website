A card game with nothing to buy

LastOne! is a colourful card game for two to four players: match the colour or the number, hit the next player with a Skip or a Draw Two, and shout "Last One!" when you are down to one card. It runs on desktop, on Android and in the browser, in English and Arabic.

It is free all the way down. There are no ads, no payments and no subscriptions, and every feature is there for everyone. It asks for no personal data either: you can play as a randomly named guest, or register a username and password — no email — to keep your rating across devices.

A table that feels real

The cards sit on a lit 3D table with felt, a wooden racetrack, a leather rail and soft shadows, and they are printed like real card stock. Each seat shouts its calls in its own voice — "Last One!", "Skip!", "Draw four… Blue!" — and in Arabic the menus run right to left, with their own font and their own callouts.

Bots with personalities

Playing alone, you face Easy, Medium or Hard opponents, and each one plays like its character: Turbo Toast throws action cards early, Professor Pickle saves his wilds, Count Crumble catches nearly every forgotten Last One!. New players get a guided first round against Captain Kiwi, with a coach pointing at each move.

Fair when it is online

In online games the server runs the game, so nobody can peek at hands or stall the table, and the daily challenge — the same deal for everyone, one try a day — cannot have its scores faked. The rules are pinned by shared test vectors, so the game on your device and the server can never disagree about what is legal. If someone drops out, a bot holds their seat until they come back. The game is built with Godot 4, and the online side runs serverless on Cloudflare Workers.

Playable by more people

Every colour also has a shape — ▲ red, ● yellow, ■ green, ◆ blue — and a colour-blind mode names the colour to match. There are three text sizes, a log of recent moves in plain words, and the web version installs like an app, including on iPhone and iPad.

LastOne! is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mattel.
