ʿilm al-farāʾiḍ  ·  the science of the ordained shares

Mīrāth

Work out how an estate divides among surviving heirs under Sunni law — with the fixed shares, the blocking rules, the arithmetic, and the text each portion rests on.

١

The estate

Burial, then debts, then the bequest — heirs are paid last, from what survives all three.
٢

The heirs

Count only those alive at the moment of death. Nearer relatives bar remoter ones automatically.
Load a classical problem
٣

The division

Every share is stated as a fraction of the base, a percentage, and an amount.
Read this before relying on it A calculator cannot replace a qualified farāʾiḍī or a competent court. It knows nothing of the facts that decide real estates: who predeceased whom, whether a marriage or lineage is established, jointly held property, an heir barred by homicide or difference of religion, an unborn child, a missing person, an estate settled under a statute that departs from the classical rules. Treat the output as a study aid and have any actual distribution confirmed by a scholar.

Shares follow the Qurʾan (4:11, 4:12, 4:176), the rulings preserved in the Sunnah, and the settled positions of the Ḥanafī, Mālikī, Shāfiʿī and Ḥanbalī schools. Where the schools diverge — the grandfather against the brothers, the donkey problem, the return of a surplus — switching schools above changes the arithmetic and the reason is stated with the result. Distribution among the distant kindred follows the Ḥanafī ahl al-qarāba method and is the least settled part of the discipline.
